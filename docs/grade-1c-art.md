# Grade 1C map artwork

The ocean texture is generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The existing penguin and lesson-island sprites are retained.

- Final asset: `public/1c/art/polar-water-tile.webp`
- Generated source: 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG, retained in the generated-image output.
- Web delivery: WebP, quality 84, with no creative alteration after generation.
- Rendering: full map width, repeated vertically, positioned at the top. The source uses even illumination and matching edge features instead of a framed scene.
- Verification: inspected a 2 × 2 repeat before integration; the week-banner shadows are shared across all weeks.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless repeating ocean texture for the learncn.fun children's penguin ice-island game. Create a brand-new square 1024x1024 tile of clear bright turquoise and azure arctic seawater viewed straight down, matching polished softly painted 3D children's game art. Gentle irregular ripples, soft luminous water caustics, subtle low-poly facets and tiny scattered sparkles. Sparse tiny white-blue ice chips, spread irregularly, leaving plenty of clear water for UI islands. Completely even diffuse illumination throughout: no bright central spotlight, no dark top or bottom band, no horizon, no shoreline or frame, no large ice islands, no characters, no text. CRITICAL: genuinely seamless texture, with continuous matching colors, wave shapes, lighting and ice-chip positions across all four edges; it must tile both horizontally and vertically without visible seams, borders, abrupt brightness changes, or obvious stripes. Calm small-scale texture, medium contrast, suitable behind overlaid text cards. Do not draw a tile grid or multiple panels: output only one single seamless square texture.
